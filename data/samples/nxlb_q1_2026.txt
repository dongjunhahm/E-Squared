NEXUS LABS (NYSE: NXLB)
Q1 2026 Earnings Release
May 5, 2026

Nexus Labs reported first-quarter 2026 results.

Highlights
- Revenue: $482 million (+4% YoY)
- GAAP EPS: $0.41
- Non-GAAP EPS: $0.62
- R&D as % of revenue: 28%

Outlook
Nexus expects Q2 revenue of $490–$510 million and maintains a cautious stance on second-half acceleration. Gross margin guidance is 62–63%.

Risks
Dependency on a small set of hyperscale customers, wafer allocation constraints, and export-control uncertainty remain primary risks.
