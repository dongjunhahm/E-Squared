ACME CORP (NASDAQ: ACME)
Q1 2026 Earnings Release
April 28, 2026

Acme Corp today reported financial results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026.

Financial Highlights
- Revenue: $1,240 million, up 6% year over year
- GAAP diluted EPS: $1.12
- Non-GAAP diluted EPS: $1.35 (excludes $0.23 of stock-based compensation and amortization)
- Gross margin: 41.2%
- Operating cash flow: $210 million

Guidance
For the second quarter of 2026, Acme expects revenue of $1.26 billion to $1.30 billion and non-GAAP EPS of $1.30 to $1.38. Full-year 2026 revenue growth is expected in the mid-single digits. Management remains constructive on demand in enterprise software and cloud infrastructure.

Risk Factors
Results may be impacted by elongated enterprise sales cycles, foreign exchange volatility, and elevated customer concentration in the top ten accounts. Macro uncertainty could pressure near-term deal timing. Supply chain lead times for networking hardware remain elevated versus pre-2024 norms.

CEO Comment
"We executed well in Q1 with solid sequential momentum," said Jordan Hale, CEO. "Customers continue to prioritize productivity and AI-adjacent workloads, though buying committees remain cautious on large transformational deals."
