NEXUS LABS (NYSE: NXLB)
Q2 2026 Earnings Release
August 4, 2026

Nexus Labs reported second-quarter 2026 results.

Highlights
- Revenue: $528 million (+9% YoY), ahead of guidance
- GAAP EPS: $0.38
- Non-GAAP EPS: $0.71
- R&D as % of revenue: 29%

Outlook
Nexus expects Q3 revenue of $535–$555 million and now sees full-year growth in the high-single digits versus prior mid-single-digit expectations. Gross margin guidance is 61–62%, modestly lower on mix.

Risks
Hyperscale concentration, wafer allocation constraints, export-control uncertainty, and rising competitive intensity in inference accelerators. Management noted a larger-than-usual backlog of design-win qualifications that could slip into 2027.
